About IREM

Musicians and engineers working across software, fabrication, and acoustic research.

Institut de Recherche et Exploration Musicale

Founded in Montréal in 2025, IREM works across music, engineering, and fabrication. A grant from the Conseil des arts et des lettres du Québec (CALQ) in late 2024 helped the team establish the organization.

We develop software, build 3D-fabricated prototypes, and conduct acoustic research for clients and institutional partners.
